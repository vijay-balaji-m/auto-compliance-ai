ASIL (Automotive Safety Integrity Level) is a risk classification system defined in ISO 26262.

It is used to determine the safety requirements of automotive systems based on:
- Severity (S): Impact of failure
- Exposure (E): Frequency of occurrence
- Controllability (C): Ability of driver to avoid harm

ASIL levels:
- ASIL A (lowest risk)
- ASIL B
- ASIL C
- ASIL D (highest risk)

Example:
A braking system typically requires ASIL D due to high severity and low controllability.
